Customer Support Terms of Service

Welcome to Miles of Smiles Customer Support. We are committed to providing excellent customer service.

REFUND POLICY
We offer full refunds within 30 days of purchase. To request a refund, please contact our support team with your order number. Refunds will be processed within 5-7 business days.

RETURNS
If you are not satisfied with your purchase, you may return items within 30 days in their original packaging. Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer unless the item was defective.

SHIPPING
Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping (2-3 business days) is available for an additional fee. Free shipping is available for orders over $50.

WARRANTY
All products come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty. Defective items will be replaced free of charge within the warranty period.

CONTACT INFORMATION
For support inquiries, please email support@milesofsmiles.com or call 1-800-MILES-SMILE. Our support hours are Monday-Friday, 9 AM - 5 PM EST.

PRIVACY POLICY
We respect your privacy and will never share your personal information with third parties without your consent. All transactions are secured using industry-standard encryption.

PAYMENT METHODS
We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and Apple Pay. All payments are processed securely through our payment partners.

LOYALTY PROGRAM
Join our loyalty program and earn points for every purchase. Points can be redeemed for discounts on future orders. Members also receive exclusive early access to sales and promotions.

SUBSCRIPTION SERVICES
If you subscribe to our monthly subscription service, you can cancel anytime. You will continue to receive benefits until the end of your current billing period. No refunds are provided for unused portions of the subscription period.
